 I Ching by Richard Wilhelm, rendered into English by C. F.

 An Illuminated I Ching by Judy Fox, Karen Hughes and John
   Tampion. Published by Neville Spearman of Suffolk and
   Princeton University Press.
 As you will know from all that I have written, I believe that
 the vital essence of the I Ching exists in its images, and the
 more words of explanation one uses, the further from that

 clarifications of Wen and Chou cannot be dispensed with.
 Finding no practical version of their work in English, I found
 it necessary to produce one. I wish you all the delight that I
